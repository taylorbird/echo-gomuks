## Fixes

- Scrolling down to the newest message now marks the room as read. Before, a room could stay unread until another message arrived.

## YouTube

- Only one video plays at a time. Starting a video stops the one already playing.
- Shorts show in a tall frame.
- If a video has been removed or made private, the link shows its normal preview.
- Video titles and thumbnails load when the video comes into view.

## Widgets

- The window where a widget asks for permissions has a new layout. Each permission has a plain description and its own switch.
