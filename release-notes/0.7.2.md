## Direct messages

- Unread DMs appear as round avatars at the top of the open space in the sidebar, up to three at a time. Turn this off with **Unread DMs in the sidebar** in Appearance settings.
- An unread DM is marked with a red dot in the room list and on its sidebar avatar. A mention inside a DM still shows the full red badge. Turn this off with **Use mention notification colors for DMs** to give DMs the same colors as rooms.

## Messages

- YouTube links show a player with the video's title, channel and thumbnail. Click it to play the video in the chat. Turn this off with **Embed YouTube videos** in Media settings.

## Known issue

- Read receipts aren't always sent, so a room can stay unread after you've read it. Marking it read in another client clears it.
