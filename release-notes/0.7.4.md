## Profiles

- Clicking someone's picture in the timeline, or their name in the member list, opens their profile as a card over the room instead of a side panel.
- The card shows their name, user ID, pronouns, local time and bio, with **Message** (or **Start chat**), **Nickname** and **Picture** buttons.
- **Shared rooms**, **Security** and **In this room** are rows you can open for more detail.
- Buttons next to the user ID copy the ID or a link to the profile.
- **View global profile** and **View member event** are under the **…** button. **Ignore** is at the bottom of the card.

## Nicknames and pictures

- You can give anyone a nickname or a picture that only you see. It replaces theirs everywhere in echo and follows you to your other devices.
- A nickname or picture you set is marked **[override]** on their profile.
- Mentions you send still use their real name.
- To set a picture, choose a file, drag one onto the picture, or paste a screenshot.

## Fixes

- Choosing a picture in a format echo can't use, such as an SVG, now shows a message instead of a broken preview.
